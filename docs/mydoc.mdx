This page hides the right-side Table of Contents (TOC). 
It gives the main body layout more reading width.

## Remove the TOC
- To remove the TOC, add the `hide_table_of_contents: true` frontmatter to your MDX file.

## Remove the Sidebar
- To remove the sidebar, add the `hide_sidebar: true` frontmatter to your MDX file.
